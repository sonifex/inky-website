What Inky does

Your child describes a picture, Inky checks that the request is suitable for children, and an AI image model draws an original black-and-white colouring page that you can print.

  • Pages are drawn by AI, so they won't always be perfect. Now and then a picture may not match what was asked for, or may have small mistakes. Please have a look before handing a page to your child.
  • Inky won't draw some requests, such as anything scary, violent, unkind or meant for adults. It also draws original pictures rather than well-known characters from films, TV, games or books, and suggests a fun alternative instead.

Pages, subscriptions and page packs

  • Free pages: new devices get 3 free pages, then a new free page every week. Free pages don't build up: you can hold one weekly page at a time.
  • Subscriptions give 60 pages a month, shared by everyone using the device. Monthly pages that aren't used don't carry over to the next month. Yearly subscribers also get 60 pages each month.
  • Page packs are one-off purchases. Pack pages never run out.
  • Free redraws: if you don't like a page, you can ask Inky to draw it again once, free of charge.
  • Failed pages are refunded. If a page can't be drawn, the page you spent is given back automatically.
